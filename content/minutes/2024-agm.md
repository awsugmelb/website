## Minutes

## Melbourne AWS User Group Inc

## Annual General Meeting

### 4th April 2024 at 10:35am
### Held online via Zoom

#### Attendees

Arjen Schwarz (AS), Matt Merriel (MM), Rob Amos (RA), Dawn E Collett (DEC), Jason Wood (JW)

#### Apologies

Rob Linton, Chris Coombs, Guy Morton, Jean-Manuel Becker

#### Resolutions

1. The minutes read from the 2023 Melbourne AWS User Group Inc Annual General Meeting were true and accurate. RA, DEC.
2. There were no applicants for membership this year.
3. It was accepted that Melbourne AWS User Group Inc. will not charge for membership. AS, MM.
4. Received and accepted the report of the Melbourne AWS User Group Inc for 2023, AS, JW.
5. Received and accepted the financial statement for Melbourne AWS User Group Inc for 2023. MM, DEC.
6. With one nomination Arjen Schwarz was declared president of Melbourne AWS User Group Inc. MM, RA.
7. With one nomination Dawn E. Collett. was declared Vice President of Melbourne AWS User Group Inc. AS, MM.
8. With one nomination Rob Amos was declared Secretary of Melbourne AWS User Group Inc. AS, JW.
9. With one nomination Matt Merriel was declared Treasurer of Melbourne AWS User Group Inc. AS, DEC.
10. With one nomination Jason Wood will join the committee as a normal committee member. AS, RA.

**Meeting closed at 10:59pm**
