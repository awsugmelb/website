## Minutes

## Melbourne AWS User Group Inc

## Annual General Meeting

### 23rd April 2025 at 12:38PM
### Held online via Zoom

#### Attendees

Arjen Schwarz (AS), Matt Merriel (MM), Rob Amos (RA), Dawn Cooper (DC)

#### Apologies

Jason Wood

#### Resolutions

1. Received and accepted the report of the Melbourne AWS User Group Inc for 2024, AS, RA.
2. It was accepted that Melbourne AWS User Group Inc. will not charge for membership. AS, RA.
3. Received and accepted the financial statement for Melbourne AWS User Group Inc for 2024. MM, AS.
4. With one nomination Arjen Schwarz was declared president of Melbourne AWS User Group Inc. RA, MM.
5. With one nomination Jason Wood was declared Vice President of Melbourne AWS User Group Inc. RA, MM.
6. With one nomination Rob Amos was declared Secretary of Melbourne AWS User Group Inc. AS, MM.
7. With one nomination Matt Merriel was declared Treasurer of Melbourne AWS User Group Inc. RA, AS.
8. Dawn Cooper has given notice that they are unable to continue as a member of this association. DC, AS.

**Meeting closed at 12:55pm**
